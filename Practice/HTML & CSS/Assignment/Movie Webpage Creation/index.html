Bahubali

Actors | Quotes
Bahubali is an epic Indian action drama film directed by S. S. Rajamouli. The story follows Mahendra Bahubali as he discovers his royal heritage and fights against the evil king Bhallaladeva to reclaim the kingdom of Mahishmati.

Actors

Prabhas played Bahubali, Rana Daggubati played Bhallaladeva, Anushka Shetty played Devasena, Tamannaah Bhatia played Avantika, and Sathyaraj played Kattappa.
